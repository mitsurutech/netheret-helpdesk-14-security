Privacy notice

How the Netheret help desk uses information about you, as the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 require us to explain.

Netheret is a made-up company in a teaching example, so this notice is an example too.

Who we are

Netheret IT Support Solutions runs this help desk and is the controller of the personal data in it.

What we keep

  • Your name, work email address and, for customers, your company.
  • A scrambled copy (a hash) of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • The support requests, comments and updates you write, and when you wrote them.
  • For staff: the jobs you are booked on and the notes you write.
  • Your accessibility settings (text size and contrast).

Please do not put passwords, bank details or health information in a support request.

Why we use it

To provide the IT support your company has a contract with us for, and to plan and improve that service, for example by counting how quickly requests are resolved. Our reports count requests; they do not judge customers.

Who can see it

  • Customers see their own company's requests and only the updates marked for them.
  • Netheret staff see the requests they need for their work. Internal notes are for staff only.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Requests are kept for two years after they are closed, so we can spot problems that come back, then deleted. Accounts are deleted when a customer's contract ends or a member of staff leaves.

Cookies

We use one cookie, the session cookie, which keeps you signed in and protects our forms. It is strictly necessary, so the law does not need us to ask first. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. Ask the help desk. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
